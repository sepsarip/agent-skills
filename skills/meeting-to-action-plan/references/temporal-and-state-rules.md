# Temporal Normalization, State Overwrite & Grounding Rules
*Grounded in Asthana et al. (arXiv:2307.15793), Golia & Kalita (arXiv:2312.17432), QMSum (arXiv:2104.05938), and EvoWiki State Resolution.*

## 1. Locate-then-Extract & Multi-Utterance Back-Linking
Never summarize a meeting from memory in a single pass:
1. **Locate**: Pinpoint the exact `[HH:MM:SS - HH:MM:SS]` span where a commitment or decision occurs.
2. **Context Window ($\pm 3$ Turns)**: Inspect the 3 speaker turns immediately preceding and following the target utterance. Often, Speaker A proposes the task/deadline in Turn $t-1$, and Speaker B simply says *"Okay, I'll do it"* in Turn $t$.
3. **Verbatim Quote**: Every extracted task and decision must store the exact spoken sentence(s) so users can verify context via tooltip or audio playback.

---

## 2. Dynamic State Overwriting (Intra-Meeting Evolution)
Meetings frequently involve proposals that get modified or rejected later in the discussion.
To prevent **Stale Retrieval**:
* Map the flow: `Proposal ➡️ Debate / Tension ➡️ Final Resolution`.
* When a decision or deadline at $T_1$ (`[00:10:15]`) is overridden at $T_2$ (`[00:45:30]`):
  * Mark the $T_1$ entry as **`[OVERTURNED]`** with citation `@[00:10:15 -> 00:45:30]`.
  * Mark the $T_2$ entry as **`[ACTIVE]`** with citation `@[00:45:30 - 00:46:05]`.
* Only `[ACTIVE]` items may enter the committed Action Plan table and JSON dispatcher.

---

## 3. Coreference Resolution Rules
Spoken transcripts are full of pronouns (*"it"*, *"that"*, *"ini"*, *"itu"*, *"dia"*).
* ❌ **Forbidden Task Name**: `"Fix that issue before Friday"` or `"Benerin bagian itu besok"`
* ✅ **Resolved Task Name**: `"Patch the Redis connection timeout in the checkout service"`
* Every `task_name` must explicitly name the **Action Verb + Specific Target Component/Deliverable + Expected Outcome (Definition of Done)**.

---

## 4. Relative Temporal Date Normalization
Always resolve relative time expressions against the Meeting Anchor Timestamp ($T_{\text{meeting}}$) generated by `parse_transcript.py`:
* **"Today / Hari ini"** $\rightarrow T_{\text{meeting}}$
* **"Tomorrow / Besok"** $\rightarrow T_{\text{meeting}} + 1\text{ day}$
* **"This [Weekday] / [Hari] ini"** $\rightarrow$ The upcoming `[Weekday]` within the current ISO week.
* **"Next [Weekday] / [Hari] depan"** $\rightarrow$ The `[Weekday]` in the *following* calendar week.
* **"End of Week (EOW)"** $\rightarrow$ Friday of the current week at `17:00:00`.
* If no deadline is stated or inferable, set `due_date_iso` to `"TBD"` and route the task to **`[NEEDS-REVIEW]`**.

---

## 5. Cognitive-Friendly Auto-Scheduling Rules
When scheduling follow-up meetings or work blocks:
1. **Context-Aware Buffer**: Always insert a **15-minute recovery buffer** before/after scheduled meetings (e.g., a 1-hour slot is booked as `10:00 - 10:45` with `10:45 - 11:00` protected as cognitive buffer).
2. **Hyperfocus Protection**: Avoid scheduling routine sync meetings inside core deep-work windows (`09:00 - 12:00` local time) unless explicitly requested in the transcript.
3. **Pre-Read & 3-Point Agenda**: Every follow-up meeting invite must include a clear Objective and a 3-point Agenda derived from unresolved meeting tensions.
